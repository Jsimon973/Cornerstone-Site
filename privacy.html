Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Who We Are

Cornerstone Housing and Development ("Cornerstone," "we," "us," or "our") is a real estate investment company based in New Jersey. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you contact us, use our website, or communicate with us by phone, text, or email.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide — name, phone number, email address, and mailing or property address.
  • Property information you share about a home you want to sell or a deal you want to discuss.
  • Communications — the content of messages, calls, and emails between you and us.
  • Basic website data such as pages visited, collected through standard analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide offers on properties.
  • To communicate with you about buying, selling, or referring properties, including follow-ups and scheduling.
  • To send you text messages you have consented to receive (see our SMS Terms).
  • To operate, improve, and secure our business and website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to run our business — for example, with service providers who help us operate (such as our phone/SMS provider or email tools), or when required by law. We never sell your personal information, and we never share your SMS opt-in or consent data for third-party marketing.

Text Messaging (SMS)

If you provide your phone number and consent, we may send you text messages related to buying, selling, or referring properties. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP. See our full SMS Terms & Conditions for details.

Your Choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to unsubscribe from SMS messages.
  • Ask us to update or delete your information by contacting us using the details below.
  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link provided or by contacting us.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your data protected.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent version.
