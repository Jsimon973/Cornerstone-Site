The Closing Process

We can typically close in 7–14 days once a purchase agreement is signed. Title search and closing paperwork are handled by a licensed title company. If you need more time before closing, we can accommodate that too.

All closings are conducted through a licensed New Jersey title company. This protects you as the seller and ensures everything is handled professionally and legally. We coordinate everything — you simply show up to sign.

None. We cover standard closing costs. There are no realtor commissions, no service fees, no hidden charges. The offer we make is the amount you walk away with at closing (minus any existing mortgages or liens that are paid off at closing).

Payment is made via certified check or wire transfer at closing, coordinated through the title company. Funds are typically available the same day as closing.
