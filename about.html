Our Story

Built on Trust & Transparency

Cornerstone Housing and Development was founded with a single mission: to give homeowners a better, faster, and fairer way to sell their property. We've seen how stressful the traditional home sale process can be — months of waiting, costly repairs, uncertain buyers — and we built our company to eliminate every one of those pain points.

Over the past 9+ years, we've purchased more than 200 homes across New Jersey. We focus primarily on our local market so we can truly understand the neighborhoods we work in and deliver results that make a real difference for sellers.

Every homeowner who reaches out to us deserves an honest conversation, a fair offer, and a smooth closing. That's the Cornerstone promise — and it's one we've kept since day one.
