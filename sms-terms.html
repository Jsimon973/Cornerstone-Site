SMS Terms & Conditions

Our text messaging program — consent, frequency, and how to opt out.

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Program Description

Cornerstone Housing and Development offers a text messaging program to communicate with property owners, real estate agents, and business contacts regarding buying, selling, and referring investment properties, including responding to inquiries, follow-ups, and scheduling calls or appointments.

Consent to Receive Messages

By providing your mobile phone number to Cornerstone Housing and Development — through our website, in person, by phone, or in the course of a business relationship — you agree to receive text messages from us at that number. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Cornerstone Housing and Development does not charge for the messages, but your carrier's standard rates apply.

To opt out: Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will receive a confirmation and will not receive further messages unless you opt back in.

For help: Reply HELP, or contact us at (848) 244-8994 or info@cornerstonehousingnj.com.

Opting Out

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by texting STOP. After you send STOP, we will send a reply confirming you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive messages from us. To rejoin, sign up as you did originally or reply START, and we will resume sending messages to you.

Help

If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program, reply with the keyword HELP for assistance, or reach us directly at (848) 244-8994.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We respect your privacy. We will not sell or share your mobile information or SMS opt-in/consent data with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
